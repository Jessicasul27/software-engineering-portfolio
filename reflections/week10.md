&#x20;  **Perspective**: Improve maintainability of the codebase from the developers POV.

4\. **Goal:** Improve the maintainability of the codebase over the next two quarters.

**3 Questions:**

How well does the system adapt to environmental changes? How well does the system handle improving and adding features?  How well does the system prevent future faults?



5\. **How well does the system adapt to environmental changes + How well does the system handle improving and adding features: ANSWER: Time spent on problems:** Measure the maintainability of the system  based on how long is spent on fixing the problems.

**How well does the system prevent future faults: ANSWER:**  **MTTR (Mean Time to Repair, Restore, Resolve or Remediate):** Measures the average time to fix or recover a 	system after failure to correct it.
